# 🃏 FizzBuzz Specialty Cards System

Specialty Cards are fun, strategic power-ups and party modifiers that players can draw, hold, or activate during FizzBuzz matches to turn the tide, sabotage opponents, or dodge drinking penalties!

## 🎴 Detailed Card Breakdowns

### 1. 👁️ Double Vision

- **Rarity**: Epic 🟣
- **Target Minigames**: _Tapping Race, Scrabble, Math Problem_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Activating this card doubles all score points earned during the round.
  - **Party Penalty Twist**: If you lose the round, your drink penalty is doubled from 1 drink to 2 drinks!

---

### 2. ⏱️ Freeze Frame

- **Rarity**: Legendary 🟡
- **Target Minigames**: _Math Problem, Trivia, Perfection, Cyclone_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Freezes the match timer for 3 seconds, granting you extra time to think, solve a complex equation, or place remaining shapes into slots.

---

### 3. 🛡️ Shield of Sobriety

- **Rarity**: Rare 🔵
- **Target Minigames**: _All Minigames_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Play when you lose a minigame. Completely cancels your drink assignment and passes the drink penalty to the player sitting on your left!

---

### 4. 🚀 Turbo Pump

- **Rarity**: Common 🟢
- **Target Minigames**: _Balloon Inflate, Tapping Race, Screen Painting_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Supercharges your inputs for 3 seconds: every tap inflates the balloon faster, paints a larger area, or steps further in the tapping race.

---

### 5. 💣 Hot Potato Pass

- **Rarity**: Epic 🟣
- **Target Minigames**: _Hot Potato_
- **Special Ability**:
  - When holding the ticking potato, instantly bypass normal turn order and throw the bomb directly to a specific target player of your choice!

---

### 6. 🧠 Mind Reader

- **Rarity**: Epic 🟣
- **Target Minigames**: _Rock Paper Scissors, Trivia_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Reveals a 0.5-second ghost preview of your opponent’s Rock/Paper/Scissors selection before lock-in, or eliminates 1 incorrect Trivia option.

---

### 7. 🎨 Paint Roller Chaos

- **Rarity**: Rare 🔵
- **Target Minigames**: _Screen Painting_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Expands your paint brush radius by +50% for 5 seconds, enabling massive screen coverage with each stroke.

---

### 8. ⏳ Time Rewind

- **Rarity**: Legendary 🟡
- **Target Minigames**: _Cyclone, Lumber Cut, Perfection_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Rewinds a missed timing hit in Cyclone or Lumber Cut, allowing 1 second-chance retry without penalty.

---

### 9. 🔀 Wildcard Swapper

- **Rarity**: Rare 🔵
- **Target Minigames**: _Scrabble, Simon Says_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Instantly swaps your current Scrabble letter rack for a fresh draw of tiles, or slow-flashes the current Simon Says pattern sequence again.

---

### 10. 🎰 Wheel Re-Roller

- **Rarity**: Common 🟢
- **Target Minigames**: _Wheel Selection Phase_
- **Special Ability**:
  - Allows you to veto a drawn 1v1 match or game category on the wheel phase, forcing a single re-spin for the lobby.
